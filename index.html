eVision Projects for Power BI: installation and setup guide

eVision Projects is a Power BI app that reports on project performance from Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central with the Eque2 eVision extension. It covers budget against actual, committed and accrued costs, margin, project sales, retentions, subcontractors, purchase commitments and plant over-runs.

This guide explains how to install the app, connect it to your own Business Central environment and company, and share it with colleagues.

Before you start

You will need the following.

Power BI

  • Licence: the person installing the app needs a Power BI Pro or Premium Per User (PPU) licence. Installing the app creates a new workspace, which a free licence cannot do.
  • Viewing the reports: each person viewing the app also needs a Pro or PPU licence, unless the installed workspace is assigned to a Power BI Premium or Fabric F64 (or larger) capacity. In that case, users with a free licence can view it.
  • Tenant settings: your Power BI administrator must allow you to install template apps. In the Fabric admin portal, go to Tenant settings → Template app settings and enable Install template apps. If you are installing from a direct link rather than Microsoft AppSource, Install template apps not listed in AppSource must also be enabled for you.
  • Azure Maps: the app includes a map visual. Your Power BI administrator must enable the Azure Maps tenant settings for the map to display.

Business Central

  • A Business Central environment with the Eque2 eVision extension installed.
  • A Business Central user account to connect with. This account needs:
    • a Business Central licence
    • the EVISION PBI REPORTS permission set (caption EVision PowerBI Reports)
    • read access to project, customer, item, resource, G/L account and purchasing data (for example, the D365 READ permission set)

The app reads data only. It does not change anything in Business Central.

Install the app

  1. Open the eVision Projects listing on Microsoft AppSource.
  2. Select Get it now and sign in with your work account if prompted.
  3. In Power BI, select Install.
  4. When installation finishes, select Go to app.

The app opens with sample data so you can explore the reports before connecting your own data. A banner at the top of the page shows that you are viewing sample data.

Connect your data

  1. In the app, select Connect your data on the banner at the top of the page.
  2. Enter the two parameters below, then select Next.
    ParameterWhat to enterExample
    ENVIRONMENTThe name of your Business Central environmentProduction
    COMPANYThe name of your Business Central company, exactly as it appears in Business CentralCRONUS UK Ltd.
  3. For Authentication method, choose OAuth2, then select Sign in and connect.
  4. Sign in with the Business Central user account described in Before you start.
  5. Power BI loads your data. The sample data banner disappears when the refresh is complete. This can take several minutes, depending on how much data you have.

Finding your environment name

  • From the browser address bar: when you are signed in to Business Central, the address looks like https://businesscentral.dynamics.com/<tenant>/<environment>/. The part after your tenant is the environment name.
  • From Business Central: select the ? (Help) icon, then Help & Support. The environment name is shown under Troubleshooting.
  • From the Business Central admin centre: the Environments list shows every environment name.

Finding your company name

In Business Central, search for Companies and open the page. Copy the Name exactly, including spaces and punctuation. The company name is case-sensitive.

Refresh schedule

By default, Power BI refreshes the data once a day. To change this, open the app's workspace, find the semantic model, select Settings and update Scheduled refresh.

Share the app with colleagues

The installed app belongs to the workspace it was installed into. To give colleagues access:

  1. Select the pencil icon in the top right of the app to open the workspace.
  2. Select Update app, then the Audience tab.
  3. Add the people or groups who should see the app, then select Update app.

Each person needs a Power BI licence as described in Before you start.

Project access control

eVision can restrict which projects each user sees. The app follows the same rules, using a security role called UserAccess in the semantic model.

  • Users with job user access enabled in eVision see only the projects they are assigned to.
  • Users with job user access disabled, or with no job user access setup, see all projects.

The app matches people by their sign-in name. The User Name in Business Central must match the part of the person's work email address before the @. For example, jsmith@contoso.com matches the Business Central user JSMITH.

To apply project access control:

  1. In the app's workspace, find the semantic model, select More options (…), then Security.
  2. Select the UserAccess role and add the people or groups who use the app.
  3. Select Save.
Row-level security applies only to people who view the app or have the Viewer role in the workspace. Workspace Admins, Members and Contributors see all projects.

Updating the app

When Eque2 releases a new version, Power BI shows a notification in the app and in the notifications pane. Select Get it and choose one of the following:

  • Update the workspace and the app if you have not changed any of the app's content
  • Update only workspace content without updating the app if you want to review the changes before republishing to your colleagues
  • Install another copy of the app into a new workspace if you want to keep your current version unchanged

Your parameters and sign-in details are kept. The app shows sample data until the first refresh after the update completes.

Updating overwrites the original reports. If you have customised a report, save it under a different name first.

Troubleshooting

ProblemWhat to check
You can't install the appCheck that you have a Pro or PPU licence, and ask your Power BI administrator to check the template app tenant settings.
The refresh fails with "company not found" or a similar errorCheck that the COMPANY value matches the name on the Companies page exactly, including spaces and punctuation.
The refresh fails with an environment errorCheck the ENVIRONMENT value against the environment name in Business Central or the admin centre.
The refresh fails with a sign-in or permission errorCheck that the account you signed in with has a Business Central licence, the EVISION PBI REPORTS permission set and read access to project and purchasing data.
Some reports are emptyCheck that the eVision extension is installed and up to date in the environment you connected to.
Users can't see some or all projectsCheck their job user access setup in eVision and that their Business Central user name matches their sign-in name. See Project access control.
The map doesn't displayAsk your Power BI administrator to enable the Azure Maps tenant settings.

To change the environment or company after installation, open the app's workspace, find the semantic model, select Settings and update Parameters. Then refresh the semantic model.
